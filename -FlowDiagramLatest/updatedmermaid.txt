DIAGRAM 2 — FAST SYNC SEQUENCE (user path)
================================================================================

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant U as User / dogecoin-qt
  participant FS as Fast Sync dialog
  participant CDN as HTTPS CDN latest.json + .dat
  participant SF as snapshot_fetch + WinHTTP
  participant N as Node dual chainstate
  participant P2P as Mainnet peers

  U->>FS: Settings - Fast Sync from CDN
  FS->>CDN: GET latest.json
  CDN-->>FS: url, sha256, height, hash_serialized
  FS->>SF: fetchassumeutxo / manifest
  SF->>CDN: stream GET multi-GB .dat
  SF->>SF: SHA-256 while writing
  alt digest mismatch
    SF-->>U: fail closed, delete partial
  else OK
    SF->>N: loadtxoutset
    N->>N: mapAssumeutxo check
    N->>N: activatesnapshot tip approx H
    N->>P2P: background prove genesis to H
    N->>P2P: keep tip moving after H
    N-->>U: wallet usable sooner
  end
```

```mermaid
  A[Empty or new datadir] --> B[Prefer Fast Sync / dialog]

  D --> E{File SHA-256 OK?}
  E -->|No| F[Fail closed / delete]
  F --> G[Offer classic IBD]
  E -->|Yes| H[loadtxoutset]
  H --> I{mapAssumeutxo hash_serialized OK?}
  I -->|No| F
  I -->|Yes| J[activatesnapshot tip near H]
  J --> K[Background prove over P2P]
  J --> L[Wallet usable sooner]
  K --> M[Fully trusted history]
  L --> N[New blocks via peers after H]
```
