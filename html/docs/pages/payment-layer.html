Payment layer — native DOGE for merchants

How Dogecoin Core Pro and optional business tooling fit together so people can pay and get paid in real DOGE — without wrapped tokens, EVM “Dogecoin L2s,” or a second currency.

Audience: users, merchants, contributors Locked: pure DOGE settlement Related: Pure DOGE strategy
One rule When money moves, it is native DOGE on the Dogecoin network. Business software may speed up tickets and inventory; it does not invent a replacement coin.

What users get in Core Pro today

Dogecoin Core Pro is a full wallet and node client. Merchant-oriented tools run in the wallet you control:

  • Doge Business — local invoices, dashboard, POS keypad
  • Payment QR / dogecoin: URI — customer pays with any DOGE wallet
  • Auto-watch — open invoices can mark paid when this wallet receives to that address
  • Meme Stream tips — tip goes on-chain to the creator’s address (not a bridge)
  • Keys in Core — no external “app chain” custody required for local POS

See the Core Pro UI reference for screenshots and layout.

Optional cloud business tools (GPE)

GoPastEarth (GPE) is a separate multi-tenant business platform (POS, inventory, invoices, multi-terminal workflows). It is not part of Dogecoin consensus and is not required to use Core Pro.

Core Pro (this client)GPE (optional product)
Role Wallet, node, local merchant tools, tips Cloud commerce: staff, inventory, multi-store ops
Keys In your Core wallet Settlement still targets real DOGE; custody model depends on how a merchant deploys (self-custody preferred)
Offline / simple shop Works alone Not required
Settlement asset Native DOGE Native DOGE (same rule)
Product boundary Core Pro documents what ships in this repository. GPE is described here only at the product and principles level — not server layout, credentials, or internal ops. Merchants should treat any cloud service with normal operational care (separate systems, least privilege, backups).

What we mean by “payment layer” (not VC “L2”)

In crypto marketing, “Layer 2” often means an EVM rollup, a bridge, and sometimes a wrapped asset. That is not this project’s direction.

PhraseMeaning for us
Payment / merchant layer Fast app for orders and invoices; final money on Dogecoin L1
Like card rails, not like Ethereum L2 Authorize and ring up off-chain; settle cash (DOGE) on the base ledger
Merchant messaging “Pay with Dogecoin” — not “bridge to our L2 token”

What we will not do

  • Require wDOGE or any wrap to accept Dogecoin at the counter
  • Define the Dogecoin product as an EVM / Dogeth / shard experience
  • Force Core users onto a foreign VM to spend their own DOGE
  • Mark an invoice “paid” from a client claim alone — chain / wallet visibility is the truth

How this fits the pure DOGE strategy

Competing on “Solidity and TVL” pulls the brand into foreign architecture. Competing on cash, POS, tips, and a trustworthy Core client keeps native DOGE as the obvious path. Details: Pure DOGE strategy.

For contributors

  • Local Business / POS / tips live under src/qt/ (this tree).
  • Do not add wrap-token or EVM settlement paths to Core Pro.
  • External business platforms may integrate via standard payment URIs and well-known Dogecoin wallet patterns (e.g. GigaWallet-style backends) — always with native DOGE as the settlement asset.
  • Security hygiene for node operators: Security hardening.
